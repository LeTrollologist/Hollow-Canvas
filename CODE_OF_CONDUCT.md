# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Aurelia is an open-source project built around thoughtful engineering, collaboration, and respect for the people who contribute to it.

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in the project, regardless of background, experience, identity, or level of technical expertise.

Participation in the Aurelia community is a privilege accompanied by a responsibility to treat others with consideration and professionalism.

## Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Be respectful, professional, and considerate.
- Communicate constructively, including when disagreeing.
- Welcome newcomers and help them understand the project.
- Provide actionable and technically relevant feedback.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Respect different levels of experience and technical knowledge.
- Focus discussions on ideas, implementations, and project outcomes rather than individuals.
- Respect project boundaries, maintainers, and established contribution procedures.
- Give appropriate credit for other people's work.
- Act in good faith when reporting problems or proposing changes.

Technical disagreement is encouraged. Disrespectful behavior is not.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or derogatory language.
- Deliberate humiliation, insults, or hostile targeting of participants.
- Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith participation.
- Impersonation or deliberate misrepresentation of another contributor.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
- Retaliation against individuals who report misconduct or participate in an investigation.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make participation in the project hostile or unsafe.

Disagreement with a technical decision, rejection of a contribution, or criticism of submitted code does not constitute harassment by itself.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Aurelia project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories.
- Issues and pull requests.
- Discussions and code reviews.
- Project documentation and contribution channels.
- Official community spaces associated with Aurelia.
- Public interactions where an individual is representing the Aurelia project.

The same expectations apply to maintainers, contributors, reviewers, and community members.

- The relevant GitHub issue, pull request, discussion, or other location.
- The approximate date and time.
- Any supporting screenshots or relevant context.

Reports will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible.

Please do not use public issues or pull requests to report sensitive personal information or private misconduct allegations.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the nature and severity of the behavior, enforcement actions may include:

1. **Correction:** A private or public request to stop inappropriate behavior.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning explaining the violation and expected corrective action.
3. **Temporary restriction:** Temporary removal of participation privileges or restrictions on interactions.
4. **Removal:** Removal from project spaces, blocking, or other appropriate measures for serious or repeated violations.

The maintainer may select an appropriate response based on the circumstances. Enforcement actions are not required to follow a fixed sequence.

Serious misconduct may result in immediate removal without a prior warning.

Retaliation against reporters or individuals participating in an investigation is itself a violation of this Code of Conduct.

## Responsibilities of Maintainers

Maintainers are expected to:

- Apply these standards consistently and fairly.
- Treat reports seriously.
- Protect the privacy of individuals involved where reasonably possible.
- Avoid conflicts of interest when handling reports.
- Explain enforcement decisions when appropriate.
- Maintain a professional and respectful environment.

Maintainers are also subject to this Code of Conduct.

## A Note on Technical Disagreement

Aurelia encourages rigorous technical discussion.

Contributors may disagree with architectural decisions, question implementations, challenge assumptions, and recommend alternative approaches.

Maintainers may reject contributions that do not align with the project's technical direction.

Neither technical disagreement nor a rejected contribution is inherently misconduct.

However, disagreement must remain respectful, relevant, and constructive.

## Attribution

## Closing Statement

Aurelia is a collaborative engineering project. Its quality depends not only on the software being developed, but also on the conduct of the people involved in developing it.

Treat others with respect, contribute in good faith, and help maintain an environment where good engineering can thrive.

**Thank you for being part of Aurelia.**
